# Intersection crossing: the most challenging arrivals

## Question
In scenes where the ego crosses or turns at an intersection with crossing
traffic, which arrival timings and speeds of the crossing cars make the
policy collide? Find the 5 most challenging cases, each on a different scene,
confirmed by repeats.

## Scope
Scenes tagged intersection. Retime the recorded cars (class automobile): up
to 2 s earlier or later, 0.5x to 2x speed. Replay traffic as recorded.

## Budget
About 50 runs.

## What counts
A front or side collision, or leaving the road. Near misses rank next.
