# In merge and cut-in scenes, which timings and speeds of the other cars make the VaVAM policy collide or leave the road? Find the 5 most challenging settings, each on a different scene and confirmed by repeats.

Study `hybrid_optuna_r3`: 54 kept runs on 9 scenes, varying actor_time_shift_s, actor_speed_scale; 2 runs not kept by the gate. Counts are kept runs only.

**Goal (checked by code, not the model):** 5 of 5 challenging settings confirmed, after 6 rounds.

## Answer

The study confirmed five challenging settings, each on a different scene. They are 1c45ce3a at +2 s and 1.0x (S22), 023b7fcc at -1.5 s and 1.25x (S1), 19f339ba at -1.5 s and 0.75x (S17), 096988dd at -1 s and 2x (S5), and 09a95ffa at -1 s and 1.25x (S7). Every kept run at each of these settings failed. S22 has the most repeats; the other four have only a few each, so their failure rates are only just shown to be above one half. However, only three of the five clearly show the policy's own mistakes:
- **S1 and S5:** the ego didn't brake enough for a slowing car ahead.
- **S17:** the ego braked hard for no reason, drifted left and hit a car beside it.

The other two mostly fail because of the replayed traffic, which doesn't react to the ego. At S22 the retimed cut-in car side-swipes an ego that is driving straight on its recorded path. At S7, a retimed car is already overlapping the ego when the policy takes over. Moving the cars earlier (-1 to -1.5 s) is common to most of the confirmed settings. But each scene was searched with only a few runs, so this is a pattern to check, not a map of where the policy fails.

- The failure rates at S1, S5, S7 and S17 are only just above one half. More repeats at each would pin them down.
- Whether S5 depends on the smeared rendering of the car ahead. Repeats with a check for rendering artifacts would settle it.
- Whether 054b5901 at -0.5 s and 1.25x (S2) is a real off-road case. Its failures are marked as possible artifacts and its rate is unsettled; more repeats with the trajectory artifact checked would settle it. If it holds, it could replace S7 or S22 as a policy-caused case.
- Whether 0cf6368d, 12855a41 and 0e899dd3 can be broken at all. Each setting tried there had only one or two runs; a denser search near their closest approaches (e.g. S13, S15, S11) would tell.
- Near misses were not ranked, and the study cannot tell apart cases where the ego merges from cases where another car cuts in.
