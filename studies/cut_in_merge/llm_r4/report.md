## Answer

The collision was scored at the moment of handoff, so these two cases are replay overlaps, not challenging cases for the policy. The three real cases all have the other cars shifted earlier or sped up, or both. At S1 and S10 the ego drifts sideways into a car in the next lane. At S8 the ego doesn't brake enough for a slowing lead car, but the camera showed that car as a heavy smear, so part of that failure may be the simulator's. S1 and S10 rest on only a few repeats each. Their ranges clear one half, but only just.
