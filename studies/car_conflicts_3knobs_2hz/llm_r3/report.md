## Answer

The goal was met: five settings on five different scenes each failed on every repeat (S13, S5, S26, S7, S8). All of them have the ego going faster than recorded at hand-off (1.4x, or 1.2x in S26), and four also have the key car slowed to 0.75x and running 0.5 to 1 s late. Only three of the five are clearly the policy's failures, though. In S5, S7 and S26 the policy steered left out of its lane, against a straight or right route command, and hit a car passing in the next lane, which was not the car being retimed. In S13 and S8 the replayed ego was already closing on a slower lead car before hand-off, and the policy got about half a second to react. The triage marks those crashes as unclear or not the policy's fault, so treat those two as setup failures, not proven policy weaknesses. No failure was flagged as a possible simulator artifact.
