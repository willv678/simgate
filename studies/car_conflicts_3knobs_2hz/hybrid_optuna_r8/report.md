# In cut-in and lead-vehicle scenes that pass as recorded, which combinations of the car's timing, the car's speed and the ego's speed at hand-off make the policy collide or leave the road? The aim is the 5 most challenging cases, each on a different scene, confirmed by repeats.

Study `hybrid_optuna_r8`: 59 kept runs on 10 scenes, varying actor_time_shift_s, actor_speed_scale, ego_speed_scale; 6 runs not kept by the gate. Counts are kept runs only.

**Goal (checked by code, not the model):** 3 of 5 challenging settings confirmed, after 6 rounds.

## Answer

The study confirmed 3 of the 5 cases it was asked for, each on a different scene. On 096988dd the setting was car +2 s late, car at 2x speed, ego at 1.2x (S2). On 19f339ba it was car +0.5 s late, car at 0.5x, ego at 1.4x (S19). On 187e6244 it was car +2 s late, car at 1x, ego at 1.2x (S17). Their ranges sit only just above 0.5, so these settings fail often but how often is not pinned down. In all three cases the ego, starting fast (1.2x–1.4x), slowed or braked when it didn't need to, drifted left out of its lane and sideswiped a replayed car in the next lane. That car was not the one the study retimed. Every failure was the policy's except one run in S17 where fault is unclear, and none were flagged as possible simulator artifacts. No setting failed on the other seven scenes, but most were tried only once to four times, which does not show they are safe.
