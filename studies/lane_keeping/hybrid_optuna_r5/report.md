# In straight lane-following scenes with other vehicles nearby, which combinations of sideways plan error and ego speed at hand-off make VaVAM leave its lane and hit someone or leave the road, and which 5 such cases, each on a different scene, are hardest?

Study `hybrid_optuna_r5`: 30 kept runs on 8 scenes, varying lateral_bias_m, ego_speed_scale; 1 runs not kept by the gate. Counts are kept runs only.

**Goal (checked by code, not the model):** 5 of 5 challenging settings confirmed, after 3 rounds.

## Answer

The goal was met: five settings on five different scenes failed in every kept run, and their 90% ranges lie above 0.5. They are S5 (-0.1 m, 1.4x) and S9 (-0.5 m, 1.4x), which are the best supported because they had the most repeats, then S1 (-0.5 m, 1.2x), S2 (+0.3 m, 1.2x) and S6 (-0.5 m, 1.0x). S8 (-0.5 m, 1.4x) is a sixth scene that did just as badly. Large shifts at 1.2x to 1.4x speed were the hard combinations, and the ego drifted the way the shift pointed. But S5 failed with only a -0.1 m shift and S6 failed at recorded speed, and the videos show most failures came from the policy not following a road that bends left. So the scene's curve matters as much as the knobs, even though these scenes were picked as "no turn". Most failures were leaving the road rather than hitting someone. Collisions happened in S5 (parked cars on the shoulder), S8 (a truck alongside) and S3 (a slower car in the next lane). The video review blamed the policy for every failure. Some failures at S5, S9, S2, S8 and S1 were flagged as possible simulator artifacts because the ego ended far from the recorded path. The review found the camera view clean before each drift, so that distance comes from the drift rather than causing it. Even so, these cases rest on failures the rendering check could not fully clear.

## Open

- Without zero-shift runs at the same speeds on these scenes, it is not clear how much of each failure comes from the shift and how much from the scene's left curve. Run each top setting's scene at 0 m shift and 1.0x and 1.4x speed.
- The scenes were picked as 'no turn' but several bend left with LEFT commands. Re-check whether the selection should exclude curved roads, or report them separately.
- S4 and S7 (-0.1 m, 1.4x) passed only two runs each, which is a hint rather than a rate. Their larger-shift corners (±0.5 m at 1.2x to 1.4x) were not tried.
- S10 (-0.3 m, 1.4x) is a single failure on the S9 scene. A few repeats would show whether the failure boundary in shift sits below -0.5 m there.
- Most of the 9×5 grid was never sampled, and positive shifts were tried on only one scene. The map of failing combinations is sparse.
- Near misses could not be ranked because every confirmed setting failed outright. Closest approach was not used.
- Rendering artifacts were flagged in all runs at S5, S9 and S2. Fully ruling them out would need runs checked for rendering quality, for example at speed scale 1.0, where the ego starts on the recording.
