# In pedestrian-crossing scenes, which combinations of ego speed at hand-off (0.6x–1.4x recorded) and pedestrian step-out time (±2 s) make the VaVAM policy with the linear MPC collide or leave the road? Find the 5 most challenging cases, each on a different scene, confirmed by repeats.

Study `hybrid_optuna_r3`: 29 kept runs on 10 scenes, varying ego_speed_scale, actor_time_shift_s; 2 runs not kept by the gate. Counts are kept runs only.

**Goal (checked by code, not the model):** 5 of 5 challenging settings confirmed, after 3 rounds.

## Answer

The goal was met: there are five settings on five different scenes where the policy failed in every kept run, each with a 90% range above 0.5. They are S1 (048b974e, 0.8x, +0.5 s), S2 (07981e6a, 1.4x, −1.5 s), S6 (0d4893f5, 1.2x, −2 s), S12 (18f8dbd6, 1.4x, 0 s) and S13 (1bbe02fc, 1.4x, −0.5 s). Each rests on only three or four repeats, so the ranges are wide. None of these failures was a collision with the pedestrian. In the triage, the policy drifted left or turned left against a RIGHT command and hit a car or van (S1, S2, S6, S12). In S13 it was flagged offroad while very close to the recorded path, which looks like a map-boundary artifact rather than the policy's fault. The study never ran the nominal setting (1.0x, 0 s) or neighbouring timings on these scenes. So it shows that these settings fail, mostly at higher hand-off speeds, but it does not show that the speed and pedestrian timing together cause the failures.

## Open

- The policy never hit the pedestrian in any run. Every failure was a left drift or wrong turn into another vehicle, or an offroad flag. Whether the pedestrian's timing matters at all is unsettled. To settle it, run each confirmed scene at 1.0x / 0 s, and at its confirmed speed with the pedestrian shifted to 0 s and to the opposite sign.
- The plan warned that these scenes might fail at nominal settings anyway. 048b974e, 07981e6a, 0d4893f5 and 1bbe02fc are among the six that failed at nominal in the earlier CATK run, which used reactive traffic. Nominal repeats at 1.0x / 0 s are needed to tell scene-inherent failures from effects of speed and timing.
- The 1bbe02fc offroad flags (S13) may be map artifacts. Checking the drivable-area polygon at that intersection entry, or running the same setting longer, would show whether the policy really leaves the road.
- Each confirmed case has only three or four repeats, so the ranges reach down to about 0.5. One or two more repeats per case would tighten them.
- The cleaner scenes (0e002edd, 12a09194, 0b10bce8) were barely searched, with one or two runs each, and one 0b10bce8 run was not kept (HALT). The 1.4x / +2 s hint on 18d28973 (S11) also needs repeats.
- This study cannot settle the comparison with the 10 Hz study pedestrian_ego_speed, because those results are not included here. Comparing the same settings across the two studies would test whether the 2 Hz rate cuts the left drift that dominates these failures.

## Provenance

Plan: `plan.json` (rationale: Copied from studies/pedestrian_ego_speed/plan.json (10 Hz) with the policy planning every 500 ms, so the method comparison can be repeated at 2 Hz. Scenes: these are the 10 scenes tagged pedestrian_crossing that have a recorded pedestrian key actor. Each scene's pedestrian is set in retime_tracks, so actor_time_shift_s moves only that person. actor_speed_scale stays at 1.0, so the pedestrian walks at the recorded speed. Traffic is replay, which keeps the scenario scripted as in Euro NCAP pedestrian tests. There is no delay, bias or noise.

Two other tagged scenes are left out: 095cf563 and 13a9767e. Neither has a pedestrian key actor, so a person-class retime there might hit no actor or the wrong one.

Budget: 5 rounds of 10 runs gives 50 runs, as the brief asks. The goal is top_k with k=5, high_min=0.5 and distinct scenes. With 90% ranges, each confirmed case needs about 4–5 failures out of 5 repeats, so roughly 25 runs go to confirmation and about 25 to searching the 5×9 grid. That is tight, and with partial results the study may confirm fewer than 5 cases.

- 6 of the scenes (048b974e, 07981e6a, 0caa8f1a, 0d4893f5, 18d28973, 1bbe02fc) failed at 0 delay in an earlier CATK run. That run used reactive traffic, not replay, so it may not carry over. - 0e002edd, 18f8dbd6, 12a09194 and 0b10bce8 are the cleaner tests. In 0b10bce8 the ego barely moved (8.9 m), and in 12a09194 the pedestrian stayed far away (11.5 m).
